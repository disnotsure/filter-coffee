Dabara & tumbler, our house pour

A filter coffee shop that doesn't apologize for either word.

FILTER opened on a side street off 100 Feet Road with one espresso machine, one steel filter set, and a bet that Bangalore didn't need to choose between them. Five years on, that's still the whole idea.

Our decoction is brewed the traditional way, every morning, by the same two people who started with us. The espresso beans rotate monthly, sourced from farms in Chikmagalur and Coorg. Everything on the menu is built to go with either cup.
